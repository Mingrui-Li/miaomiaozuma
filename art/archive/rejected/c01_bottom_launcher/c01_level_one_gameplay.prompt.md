## 最终提示词

```text
Use case: ui-mockup
Asset type: high-fidelity visual concept for a portrait Douyin mini game, first gameplay level
Primary request: Create a polished 9:16 portrait mobile game screen for a cute game titled 喵喵回窝. Show a clearly readable curved C-shaped paw-print track with a moving chain of five distinctly different round chibi cats. The player launches a cat from a cozy cat-bed launcher at the bottom to place three matching cats together so they happily cuddle and fly home.
Scene/backdrop: warm sunny cat courtyard, pale blue sky, cream clouds, soft green lawn, a small whimsical canned-food storeroom at the end of the track; background decorations remain subdued and never obscure gameplay.
Subjects: exactly five readable cat breeds repeated along the track: orange tabby with forehead stripes and round face; white-and-blue ragdoll with dark ears and fluffy tail; blue-gray British shorthair with small ears and wide face; calico with orange-black-white patches; black cat with purple undertone, pointed ears and crescent-shaped eyes. Each breed must differ by color, markings and silhouette even at small size.
UI composition: 750x1334 design logic, portrait. Top safe area left empty enough for device controls. HUD below safe area includes a compact pause button, level 1 badge, progress target, and a large purple purr-energy meter close to full. Main track occupies the middle 60 percent. Bottom area has a large cat-bed launcher with current cat, a clear next-cat preview, three small booster slots, and a glowing aim line ending on a pair of matching cats. Show a heart/cuddle anticipation effect where the shot will land. No banner ad.
Style/medium: premium 2D mobile game illustration, soft gummy texture, thick dark-brown outlines, subtle dimensional highlights, rounded handcrafted UI, cute but not babyish, production-ready casual game polish.
Lighting/mood: warm, optimistic, playful, satisfying.
Color palette: sky blue, cream, grass green, orange, coral, mint, blue and purple; high contrast for gameplay.
Text: Use no small body text. Only the exact large Chinese text “第1关” may appear; all other labels should be iconographic or blank so Figma can add accurate typography later.
Constraints: strong single-thumb hierarchy; track and cats instantly readable; exactly five breeds; no violence, no exploding cats, no realistic fur, no watermark, no logo from another game, no malformed UI, no gibberish text. Leave clear UI-safe margins.
```
