## 最终提示词

```text
Use case: ui-mockup
Asset type: high-fidelity three-screen visual design board for the portrait mobile game 喵喵回窝
Input image: use the attached first-level gameplay concept strictly as the style reference for materials, lighting, cat proportions, thick dark-brown outlines, color palette, and UI craftsmanship. Do not copy its exact layout.
Primary request: Create one clean landscape presentation board containing exactly three separate 9:16 portrait mobile screen concepts side by side: Home screen, Victory result, and Failure/Revive.
Home screen: warm sunny cat courtyard; the exact five established cat breeds gather around the title area; a very large orange primary button in the lower thumb zone; top fish-currency pill and settings icon; three small bottom navigation icons. The title itself may be left as a clean cream wooden sign for Figma text.
Victory screen: joyful cats jumping into cozy cardboard boxes, three large star slots with all three glowing, score and fish reward areas represented by clean icon-based rows, a large orange next-level button, smaller replay, reward-video and share buttons.
Failure/Revive screen: cute crowded canned-food storeroom with worried but unharmed cats, warm dim overlay, cream dialog, a large coral/orange revive button with video icon, smaller retry and map buttons. Keep the mood encouraging rather than sad.
Style/medium: premium 2D casual mobile game UI, soft gummy cats, subtle dimensional highlights, rounded handcrafted panels, cream cards, warm shadows, polished production mockup.
Color palette: match the reference exactly—sky blue, cream, grass green, orange, coral, mint, blue, purple, dark-brown outlines.
Text: no readable small text and no gibberish. Leave blank sign/button label areas so exact Chinese copy can be created in Figma.
Constraints: exactly three screens, equal size, fully visible with space between them; exactly five established breed designs wherever the group is shown; no real photos, no violence, no third-party logos, no ads covering play, no watermark.
```
